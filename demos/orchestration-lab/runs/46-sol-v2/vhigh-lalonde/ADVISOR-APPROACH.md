Task: independently review the proposed approach to a LaLonde NSW/CPS matching specification curve before implementation. BRIEF.md in the working directory is authoritative. No web access or package installation is allowed.

The proposed analysis uses the NSW randomized treated-minus-control difference as the experimental benchmark. It forms the observational composite from NSW treated plus CPS controls and estimates the ATT under a full 2 x 2 x 2 curve: demographics-only versus demographics plus re74/re75; no trimming versus trimming to the intersection of treated/control propensity-score ranges (with score refit); and 1-nearest-neighbor matching with replacement versus five-stratum score subclassification. The naive observational difference is also reported.

For 1-NN, the ATT is the mean matched-pair difference. Because ordinary nonparametric bootstrap inference is invalid for nonsmooth nearest-neighbor matching, the proposed interval clusters paired differences by original matched control ID (CR1; t critical value over unique matched controls), thereby accounting for reuse. For stratification, the ATT is a treated-share-weighted average of within-stratum differences with the usual stratified independent-sample plug-in variance. The script will include exact sample counts, estimates, intervals, and gaps from the experimental benchmark and generate one 300+ dpi specification plot.

Specific decision question: should the lead proceed with this design, or is there a material technical defect that must be corrected first? Focus on the estimand, trimming, strata construction, and defensibility of interval estimates. Give decisive advice; do not edit files.
