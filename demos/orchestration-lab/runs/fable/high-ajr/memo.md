# Memo: What the AJR replication stress tests support

The headline finding survives the perturbations that matter, and it does not survive the ones that were never going to test it. Sorting those two cases apart is the whole job here, so let me be precise about each.

Specifications 1 through 3 use the full 64-country sample and vary only the controls (bivariate, plus latitude, plus continent dummies). All three clear the weak-instrument screen, and the 2SLS estimate stays in a consistent, large-relative-to-OLS band of 0.84 to 1.00. The OLS-to-2SLS gap, and its direction, holds throughout. That is the part of the AJR result your evidence is entitled to call robust: robust to reasonable choices about controls, not fragile to them. One caveat belongs in the text. In spec 3 the homoskedastic first-stage F (11.01) clears the threshold but the HC1-robust F (9.27) dips just below it, so treat spec 3 as adequate rather than clean, and lean on specs 1 and 2 as the sturdier anchors.

Specifications 4 (dropping the neo-Europes) and 5 (Africa only) both fail the first-stage-strength screen, and spec 5 collapses outright (F = 0.30, coefficient 2.40 with a standard error of 3.99). Here is the sentence to get exactly right, because it is easy to state as either false reassurance or false alarm: a collapsed first stage in specs 4 and 5 does not overturn the headline finding, and it does not confirm anything about those subsamples either. The identifying variation in settler mortality was concentrated precisely in the countries those cuts remove, so dropping them strips out the instrument's leverage rather than putting the finding to a fair test. A weak first stage makes that subsample's 2SLS coefficient uninformative, not falsifying. The Africa-only estimate is not evidence for a larger effect, nor evidence against the result; it is simply not evidence, and the memo should not let it read as either.

What you may claim, then, is bounded on both sides. You may say the OLS-2SLS gap and its sign replicate across every specification where the instrument is strong. You should not claim the effect holds universally, or that it is confirmed within Africa or outside the neo-Europes, because those checks are silent rather than supportive. And because the point estimate does move across specs 1 through 3, the right register is large and robust in sign and rough magnitude, not precisely pinned down.
