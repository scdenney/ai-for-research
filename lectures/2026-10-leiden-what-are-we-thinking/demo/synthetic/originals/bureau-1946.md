## 1. Arrivals

Arrivals by official sailing in the period 1–11 June totalled 9,412 persons on
fourteen vessels. The Bureau has no count of arrivals by private boat. Reports from the
coastal police posts suggest that such landings continued at a rate the Bureau is unable
to estimate.

## 2. Suspension of sailings

Sailings from Japan were suspended from 12 June. Two orders were in force at that date:
the quarantine order issued after the cholera cases at Pusan, and the reassignment of
vessels to the transport of grain. The Bureau cannot say which of the two halted the
sailings. No instruction received by this office gives a reason.

## 3. Quarantine station

The quarantine station at Pier 2 processed all official arrivals before 12 June. Staff
report that a considerable share of persons arriving outside the official sailings
carried no certificate of any kind. The station has asked for additional staff and for
clear instruction on how such persons are to be registered.

## 4. Records

Landing registers for May are complete and have been forwarded. Registers for June
cover official sailings only. The Bureau repeats its advice of Report No. 4 that the
registers should not be used as a count of total arrivals.
