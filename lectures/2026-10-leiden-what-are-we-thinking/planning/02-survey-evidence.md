Collected 20 October 2026 (web sweep by an Opus research agent; Nature microdata recomputed locally). Every figure below was read at the primary source unless marked. The audience survey (`survey/specs/leiden26_audience.yaml`) borrows item wording from the sources marked **borrowed**; `survey/published.yaml` holds the comparison values that `survey/results.py` draws.

## Not used, for the record

- Ithaka S+R 2024 (2,654 US instructors): 42% prohibit student use; humanities 53%, social sciences 40%.
- Utrecht University 2024 (1,633 students, 348 teachers): >80% have used generative AI; news article only.
- ESB Oct 2025 (174 Dutch economists): 82% use generative AI; 51% of users daily; 31% say use should always be disclosed.
- HEPI/Kortext 2026 (1,054 UK undergraduates): 95% use AI in some way; 12% put AI text into assessed work.
- Pew June 2025 (5,123 US adults): 34% have used ChatGPT; postgraduates 52%.
- No published Leiden figures exist; a 2024 university survey on LLM chatbots in education has no public results. No survey asks doctoral supervisors about their candidates' AI use.
